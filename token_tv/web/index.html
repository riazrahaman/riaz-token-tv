      How to read this display

      Percentages show used quota. Each card is one account, showing its highest reported window; the account's other windows are listed under Other windows. Timers belong to the window shown on the card. A previous value is marked OLD and is not a current reading.

      Ollama Cloud shows the current monthly credit period's spend — from the day your account was created — against your monthly credit budget (from your plan, or the allowance you set). The budget is monthly and spend is not time-boxed, so this is a credit budget, not a weekly limit. Antigravity reports the used share of its Gemini weekly and five-hour limits, read from the Antigravity CLI. If a provider does not report quota, it stays unknown. Laptop-linked accounts need the laptop online.
